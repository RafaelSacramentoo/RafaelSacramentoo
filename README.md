### About

Product Designer working at the intersection of product, research, data and interface. Five years designing B2B SaaS and consumer products across compliance, HR tech, marketplace, insurance and online reputation.

I came from a development background before moving into product design, which is why I still live here on GitHub. Most of what I build in public is either a design tool for my own process or an interface I wanted to understand from the inside.

### Currently

- **Product Designer at Safe Space**, a B2B platform for compliance, conduct management and whistleblowing channels
- Designing AI-assisted workflows for case triage, summarisation and decision support
- Studying **Data Science** at UNINASSAU
